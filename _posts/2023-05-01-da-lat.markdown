Da Lat is a city in the Lam Dong province of Vietnam. It is located at an altitude of 1,500 meters (4,900 feet) above sea level, and is surrounded by mountains. Da Lat is known for its cool climate, its French colonial architecture, and its many lakes and waterfalls.

## Travelling to Cau Dat
Cau Dat is known for its tea plantations, which cover an area of about 230 hectares. The tea plantations are a popular tourist destination, and visitors can enjoy the beautiful scenery and learn about the tea production process.
