Phu Quy Island is a small, fishing island located about 120 kilometers off the coast of Phan Thiet, Vietnam. It is known for its natural beauty, with white sand beaches, clear blue waters, and lush green vegetation. The island is also home to a variety of wildlife, including dolphins, turtles, and birds.

Phu Quy Island is a popular destination for tourists, who come to enjoy its natural beauty and its many activities. Some of the most popular activities on the island include swimming, sunbathing, fishing, diving, and hiking. There are also a number of historical and cultural sites on the island, including the Phu Quy Lighthouse and the Phu Quy Pagoda.
