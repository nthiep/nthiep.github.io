Ta Dung Lake is a lake located in the Dak Glong District of Dak Nong Province, Vietnam. It is a popular tourist destination, known for its beautiful scenery and its many islands.

Ta Dung Lake is home to a variety of plants and animals, including many species of birds and fish. The lake is also a popular spot for swimming, boating, and fishing.

There are a number of homestays and resorts located around the lake, and there are also a number of hiking trails that offer stunning views of the lake and the surrounding mountains.
