Ba Den Mountain, also known as Nui Ba Den, is a mountain located in the center of Tay Ninh Province, Vietnam. It is the highest mountain in southern Vietnam, with an elevation of 986 meters (3,232 feet). The mountain is a popular tourist destination, and is known for its beautiful scenery, religious sites, and hiking trails.

The name "Ba Den" means "Black Lady Mountain" in Vietnamese. The mountain is said to be home to a goddess named Thien Hau, who is worshipped by many people in the region. There are several temples and pagodas on the mountain, including the Thien Hau Pagoda, which is the largest temple on the mountain.

Ba Den Mountain is a beautiful and popular tourist destination. It is a great place to hike, see the scenery, and learn about Vietnamese culture.
